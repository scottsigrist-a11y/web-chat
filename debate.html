Set up the debate

One model (Llama-3.2-1B, on your device) plays both debaters, switching sides each turn. Openings and presentation replies aim for about 1000 words each; in a question round, one side asks 5 questions and the other answers all five in about 1000 words total, then they swap. Word counts are targets, not exact — small models cannot count words precisely. Each long reply takes minutes on a phone; the debate runs itself once started, and Stop halts it between replies.
